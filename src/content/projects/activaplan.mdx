## Problem

Most workout apps want an account, a subscription, and a constant connection, which is a lot to ask just to do push-ups at home.

## Solution

ActivaPlan keeps everything on the device. You pick exercises by muscle group and level, assemble routines, schedule them across the week, and run each session with timers, rest, and history. There is no account, no server, and no tracker.

## Key features

- Home screen with today's workout, this week's streak, active program progress, and template recommendations
- Browse and filter exercises by mode, muscle, level, and keyword, with step images, tips, common mistakes, and safety notes
- Strength, cardio, or mixed routines with estimated minutes, ready-made templates, and weekly plans
- Walk and run sessions by segment, with heart-rate zones, sound and vibration cues, and optional GPS distance and pace
- Session history grouped by month with Completed, Partial, and Stopped states
- Light and dark themes, plus Indonesian and English

## Under the hood

Clean Architecture with MVVM. Logic, ViewModels, and UI live in `commonMain`; platform code covers reminders, permissions, GPS, cues, and the keep-screen-on flag. Rules like the session state machine, schedule conflicts, and streaks are pure functions, and an injectable `LocalClock` makes all date-dependent logic testable.

## Status

In active development. Most testing so far has been on Android; iOS behavior still needs to be verified on a device.
